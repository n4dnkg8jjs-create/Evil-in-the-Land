Host from Your PC

The lobby runs on the host's computer, so friends connect straight to you. No online servers or accounts.

  1. Download the game and unzip it.
  2. Install Node.js (the LTS version) if you don't have it.
  3. Double-click the start script: start-windows.bat, start-mac.command or start-linux.sh.

The game opens in your browser with a lobby already made. Give friends the code and the address it shows. They can join from this page.

    The pilgrimage

    The land is dead. At each blighted altar, kneel and pray: make the sign of the cross and hold your prayer while the swarm comes for you. The more knights pray together, the faster the prayer is answered. Those who do not pray must defend those who do.

    When a prayer is answered, the land around the altar lives again, every knight is healed, and the fallen rise.

    Dread

    Fear is its own enemy. It rises when you stand alone in the blight, when demons close in, when you bleed, and when the hooded one on the ridge is watching. As it climbs you will hear things, the edges of sight go dark, and you will see things that are not there. At its worst your hands shake. Pray, keep close to your brothers, and stand in the light of cleansed altars to master it.

    The sutler's wagon

    Demons you cut down, altars you hallow, roads you win and pages you find all earn silver. Spend it at the sutler's wagon at the start of each road: new weapons, a tempered edge, proofed harness, a blessed relic, healing draughts. What you buy is kept.

    And it gets worse the further you go. Each road deeper into the blight is darker than the last, and stranger things happen on it.

    The First Company

    Twenty knights took this road a year before you. Their graves lie beside it, and with them the pages of their chaplain, Brother Anselm. Walk up to a cairn to read what he wrote.

    Seven chapters climb from the Vale of Ashes to the mountain's peak. A full pilgrimage takes an hour to an hour and a half. Your progress is saved after each chapter.
